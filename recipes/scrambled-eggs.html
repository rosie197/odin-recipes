Scrambled Eggs

A comfoting classic

These quick to prepare eggs are the perfect way to start your day, serve on fresh sliced sourdough with a side of avocado for the perfect, easy breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 table spoon butter
    • 2-3 eggs
  • 50g grated cheese
  • Splash of milk

Steps

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl, add the splash of milk and throuroughly whisk
  2. Heat the butter in a medium sized pan over a medium heat, add the whisked eggs and gently move as the eggs cook allowing the uncooked eggs to move to the bottom of the pan.
  3. Once the eggs are 3/4 done, take off the heat, add the cheese and let the eggs finish cooking in the risidual heat.Add salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Serve over lightly toasted sourdough and smashed avocado, and finish with a drizzle of hot sauce.
