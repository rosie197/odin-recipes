Jacket Potato

A comfoting classic

A hearty and warming classic, perfect for long, cold days when you need a quick and filling meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 table spoon olive oil
  • 1 table spoon butter
    • 1 large baking potato
  • 50g grated cheese
  • 1 tin baked beans

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4
  2. Pierce the skik of the potato all over with a fork, place in microwave for 10 minutes. Once done coat with the oil and place in the oven to complete cooking for an additional 15-20 minutes, until the skin is crispy.
  3. When the potato is near done in the oven, heat the beans either in the microwave or on the stove add a touch of chilli to the beans for an extra kick.
  4. Take the potato out the oven, and cut a cross in the top and gently squeeze to open up the potato be careful as the potato will be very hot! Add the butter to the open potato and lightly mash, add half the cheese, then the beans, and then the rest of the cheese.
