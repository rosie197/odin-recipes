Veggie Lasagna

A comfoting classic

This easy to prepare lasagana is the perfect comfort food to prepare ahead of time and store in the freezer for when you need a quick dinner during a busy week.

Ingredients

  • 2 table spoons olive oil
    • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 red onion
  • 400g veggie mince
  • 300g fresh lasagna sheets
  • 800g passata
  • white sauce
  • 125g mozzarella

Steps

  1. Pre heat the oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4
  2. Dice the onion and grate the garlic
  3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat, add the onion and soften for a few minutes before adding the garlic and cooking for two more minutes
  4. Add the veggie mince and passata and cook for 10-15 more minutes. Add salt and pepper to taste, and a couple of fennel seeds for extra depth of flavour
  5. Once the mince mix is done, assemble your lasagna by adding a layer of mince mix to a baking dish followed by a layer of lasagna sheets and finally a layer of white sauce. Repeat until all mince, white sauce, and lasagna sheets have been used. Top with the mozzarella and bake for 30-40 minutes until the top is bubbling and lightly browned
  6. Let cool for 5 minutes before slicing and serving
